The Problem

What Manual QC
Was Costing

End-of-line inspection left defects hidden until it was too late — and management flying blind on efficiency.

01

Late Defect Discovery

Tears, smudges, and misalignments found only at final inspection — after full assembly cost was already incurred.

02

High Rework & Scrap

~12% of mats required rework or scrapping. Each rejection at the end of the line meant maximum material and labour loss.

03

Zero Performance Visibility

No real-time view of operator efficiency or defect trends. Supervisors relied on end-of-shift reports that were already outdated.

04

Production Bottlenecks

Manual final-stage checking became the slowest step on the line, throttling throughput under tight delivery schedules.

The Solution

Vision AI at Every
Checkpoint

Cameras at the vacuum transfer station, stitching, and branding stages — catching faults the moment they occur.

🎯

Fault Detection at Source

Cameras at the vacuum transfer station capture each mat after cutting. Shape distortions, tears, and debris trigger instant alerts before downstream operations.

📊

Operator Efficiency Tracking

Defined presence zones log active vs idle time. Each mat cleared increments the operator's count. Supervisors see live utilization and hourly output.

🔍

Multi-Checkpoint Validation

Only mats passing all vision checkpoints proceed to final packaging — eliminating the traditional bottleneck of manual end-of-line inspection.

📡

Live Supervisor Dashboard

Real-time utilization metrics and defect trend data delivered to a supervisor dashboard — enabling performance coaching, not just monitoring.

Implementation

From Assessment
to Full Rollout

A structured four-phase deployment — validated alongside manual checks before transition.

01

Assessment & Data Collection

Production stages mapped. Sample defects across cutting, stitching, and branding operations collected and annotated for model training.

02

Model Deployment on Jetson Orin Nano

Optimized vision models deployed directly onto NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano devices at cutting and stitching checkpoints for real-time edge inference.

03

Integration & Testing

Dashboards connected for live tracking. System ran in parallel with existing manual checks and validated for precision and reliability.

04

Full Rollout & Training

Operators and supervisors trained for seamless adoption. System transitioned to primary QC mechanism with zero production disruption.
